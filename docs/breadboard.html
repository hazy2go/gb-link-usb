GB Link Breadboard

Pico 2 W wired straight to the cut DMG-04, no level shifter. This is the wiring that passed first light (1000 bytes, 0 errors). Hover a wire in the list to highlight it. Hole names are column letter + row number, e.g. a3.

  1. Pico

    USB end at the top. Left pins in column c, right pins in column h, rows 3 to 22. That leaves columns a, b and i, j free for jumpers.

  2. Link cable to the Pico

    Each wire goes into a free hole on the left (a or b) of its Pico pin's row.

    green, plug 5, clocka3, pin 1 GP0
    red, plug 2, data from GBb4, pin 2 GP1
    blue, plug 6, GNDa5, pin 3 GND
    orange, plug 3, data to GBb6, pin 4 GP2

    Shield: twisted and taped, not connected. Nothing else is wired: no shifter, no power rails.

  3. Why no level shifter

    The Pico 2's RP2350 pins take up to 5.5 V while the Pico is powered (datasheet, FT pins), and the DMG reads the Pico's 3.3 V fine. This only holds for the Pico 2, not the original Pico.

  4. Power order

    Pico USB in first, then the Game Boy on. Game Boy off first, then USB out. 5 V into an unpowered Pico is not allowed.
